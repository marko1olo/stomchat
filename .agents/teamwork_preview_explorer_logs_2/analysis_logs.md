## 1. Executive Summary

An exhaustive analysis of all available runtime logs and persistence state was conducted to determine the exact triggering and silence dynamics of the StomChat AI assistant.

### Key Quantitative Findings:
1. **Severe Bot Suppression Rate (95.7% – 97.1%):**
   - Across the entire log history, only **250 total trigger events** were initiated (115 in recent logs `bot.log` + `bot.log.1` + `bot.log.2`), whereas **5,578 silence/suppression events** were recorded.
   - For every 1 message the bot attempts or sends, it is suppressed or silenced **22.3 times** (33.8 times in recent logs).
2. **The "Dialogue Stale" Trap (`count_since > 5`):**
   - Suppressed **60 direct dialogue interactions**, including **14.2% (23/162)** of all direct clinician replies in the database.
   - In multiple instances, legitimate clinical rebuttals and surgical inquiries were discarded simply because 6 casual messages (just 1 above threshold!) were posted in the main group while the doctor was typing.
3. **The "Deadlock on First Reply" Triage Bias:**
   - Accounted for **1,730 suppressions (31.02% of all silences)**.
   - 78.9% (1,323) of all negative triage decisions occurred because *"2 or more colleagues are already discussing the case"*. The moment a colleague drops a 2-word reaction ("Снимок?", "+", "Удали"), the triage LLM silences the bot, preventing evidence-based guidelines from reaching the chat.
4. **Static 120-Minute Cooldown & 10-Minute Retry Backoff:**
   - Caused **1,402 suppressions** (662 passive cooldowns + 740 retry backoffs).
   - Median time remaining when clinical messages were blocked was 71 minutes for cooldown and 6 minutes for backoff.
   - High-urgency clinical consultations (e.g., implant mobility, cross-system component incompatibility) were suppressed because 10 minutes remained on the timer.
5. **Fail-Closed Quality Validator on Infrastructure Hiccups:**
   - 155 drafts were rejected by `check_response_quality`.
   - Crucially, **66.5% (103/155) of validator rejections were NOT due to clinical flaws**, but because the secondary validator cascade timed out or hit 503 errors (`validator_unavailable: gemini cascade exhausted`). High-quality drafts generated by the primary LLM were silently discarded.
6. **Supervisor Downtime & Watchdog Resets:**
   - `bot_supervisor.log` recorded **2,224 starts and 2,123 crashes/stops** (1,954 exit code 1).
   - Key crash drivers: event loop freeze watchdog (`цикл событий не отвечает 300.0 c`) and MTProto connection timeout loops (e.g., 76-minute restart loop on 2026-09-07).

## 3. Detailed Breakdown of Silence & Suppression Causes

### 3.1 Passive Cooldown (`120 min`) & Retry Backoff (`10 min`)
- **Total occurrences:** 1,402 (662 cooldown + 740 backoff).
- **Implementation:** `assistant.py:1166–1185` (`passive_gate_block_reason`).
- **Cooldown distribution:**
  - Min: 1 min, p25: 29 min, Median: 71 min, p75: 99 min, Max: 120 min.
- **Retry backoff distribution:**
  - Min: 1 min, p25: 3 min, Median: 6 min, p75: 8 min, Max: 10 min.
- **Pathology:**
  The static 120-minute window treats a quiet Sunday night (1.5 msgs/hr) identically to a frantic weekday case review (65 msgs/hr). During peak clinical hours (11:00–23:00 MSK), between 80 and 150 messages pass between bot interventions. The bot is rendered completely unavailable when clinicians are actively posting diagnostic queries.

### 3.2 Dialogue Freshness Cutoff (`dialogue_stale`, `count_since > 5`)
- **Total occurrences in logs:** 60.
- **Database correlation:** 14.2% of all doctor replies to bot messages were silently dropped.
- **Implementation:** `assistant.py:2588`:
  ```python
  if count_since > 5:
      logger.info(f"Dialogue reply is stale. {count_since} messages have passed since bot message {ref_id}. Skipping to avoid thread hijacking.")
      return False
  ```
- **Pathology:**
  In active dental chats, 5 messages pass in under 2 minutes (18.1% of intervals) or under 5 minutes (40.5% of intervals). When a doctor takes 3 minutes to read a bot response and formulate a clinical reply (e.g. asking about dosage or challenging an anchor type), the 6th unrelated message in the chat causes the bot to abandon the consultation.

### 3.3 LLM Triage Rejections (`passive_triage_rejected`: 1,730 cases)
- **Total occurrences:** 1,730 in logs.
- **Implementation:** `assistant.py:2204–2264` (`check_llm_triage`).
- **Distribution of Triage Rejection Reasons:**
  1. `already_discussed_by_humans`: **1,323 (78.9%)**
  2. `other`: 308 (18.4%)
  3. `short_remark_thought_out_loud`: 21 (1.3%)
  4. `no_question_asked`: 13 (0.8%)
  5. `chitchat_flood_humor_nonclinical`: 11 (0.7%)
- **Triage Confidence Distribution:**
  - Confidence 1.00: 1,036 times (61.8%)
  - Confidence 0.90: 385 times (23.0%)
  - Confidence 0.80: 213 times (12.7%)
- **Pathology:**
  The triage prompt contains two fatal design flaws:
  1. *Prompt/Caller Contract Inversion:* The prompt says "Reply ONLY if there is a direct @ or reply to bot". But line 2770 explicitly guards: `if triggered and not is_dialogue:`. Mentions and replies NEVER reach `check_llm_triage`. The LLM is commanded to look for signals that are impossible to be present!
  2. *"Deadlock on First Reply":* The prompt commands: *"Если 2 или более коллег уже переписываются, спорят, отвечают друг другу — НЕ ВМЕШИВАТЬСЯ!"* In a 750-doctor chat, any post receives an instant 2-word reaction ("Хм", "Снимок?", "Удали"). The LLM categorizes this as "уже обсуждается живыми участниками" and silences the bot, even when the doctor's acute clinical question remains completely unanswered.

### 3.4 Dialogue Continuation Triage Disconnect (`dialogue_triage_rejected`: 16 cases)
- **Total occurrences:** 16 cases.
- **Implementation:** `assistant.py:236–267` (`check_dialogue_continuation_triage`).
- **Pathology:**
  The prompt instructs the LLM to inspect the last 5 messages of the main chat room (`recent_chat_str`). If users in the main room are discussing a different topic (e.g., microscope equipment, clinic scheduling), the LLM concludes "тема сместилась" (Rule 1) and aborts the doctor's ongoing private thread. Furthermore, Rule 2 stigmatizes clinical skepticism ("это спорно", "а где щель под микроскопом?") as "троллинг", causing the bot to terminate legitimate discussions.

### 3.5 Negative Feedback & Silencing Penalties
- **Total detections:** 2 events.
- **Total penalty skips:** 19 events (`bot_is_silenced`).
- **Recorded Incidents:**
  1. `bot.log.1:30721` (2026-08-31 15:49:41 UTC): Doctor wrote `'Забаньте бота😁'`. The bot imposed a 4-hour total blackout (`silenced_until: 2026-08-31T19:49:41.409559`). For 4 hours, 19 trigger attempts across all channels (mentions, referee, media) were suppressed.
  2. `bot.log.3:1610`: User wrote `'Не пиши в мой адрес. Скайнет е ый'`.
- **Pathology:** A 4-hour global blackout across the entire chat for all 750 members in response to a single doctor's joke is an excessively blunt instrument.

### 3.6 Quality Validator Failures (`check_response_quality`: 155 rejections)
- **Total rejections:** 155 (68 text drafts + 49 media drafts + 38 unavailable).
- **Categorization of Rejection Reasons:**

## 7. Actionable Architectural Recommendations (for R3)

Based strictly on log evidence, the following 5 changes are mandatory:
1. **Differentiate Direct Replies from General Dialogue Freshness:**
   - For explicit quote-replies to the bot (`reply_to_msg_id == bot_msg_id`), raise `count_since` limit from 5 to **25-30 messages**, with a time limit of 90 minutes.
2. **Dynamic Velocity-Based Cooldown:**
   - Replace the static 120m cooldown with a dynamic function ($45 \text{ min}$ during high chat velocity $\ge 50 \text{ msgs/hr}$, up to $150 \text{ min}$ during night hours).
   - Add a volume bypass: if $\ge 40$ messages passed and $\Delta t \ge 45 \text{ min}$, allow trigger.
3. **Fix Contract Inversion in `check_llm_triage`:**
   - Remove "Must have @ or reply to bot" from triage prompt.
   - Refactor Rule 1 of triage: allow intervention when human answers are incomplete, single-word, or clinically hazardous.
4. **Soft Fail on Quality Validator Outages:**
   - If the primary draft succeeded and the validator fails due to cascade exhaustion (`validator_unavailable`), do NOT discard the draft. Fallback to basic regex sanity checks (no toxic words, length $\le 1200$) and allow publication.
5. **Group Chat Emoji Sanitizer:**
   - Port the PM emoji sanitizer (`re.sub(r"[😅😂😎😤😏🤣🤡🙄]+", "", text)`) into the group assistant handler to prevent good clinical drafts from being dropped due to emojis.
